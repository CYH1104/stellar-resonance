The 54 faces and blue patterned back are rasterized from the upstream SVG files to 300px-wide WebP, quality 94. Composition and colors are unchanged. provenance.json records original paths and SHA-256 of both source and shipped bytes. Back.webp is also used inside the ivory Mahjong tile body.

All artwork is bundled and served locally. No external image requests, scripts, fonts, or player data are involved.
